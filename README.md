# Hotel-business-analysis-EDA

##1. Introduction

• In this project, I analysed hotel booking data to understand customer booking
behaviour and cancellation patterns.

• The dataset contains hotel booking information for City Hotel and Resort Hotel. The
main purpose of this analysis was to understand which hotel type customers prefer
and which booking factors are related to a higher cancellation rate.

The project focuses on the following main business questions:
1. Which hotel type receives more bookings?
2. How do booking trends change across different months?
3. Which hotel type has a higher cancellation risk?
4. Does the length of stay affect the cancellation rate?
5. Does lead time affect the cancellation rate?
6. Which combination of booking characteristics may indicate a higher risk of
cancellation?

Answering these questions can help hotel management understand customer behaviour,
improve demand planning, reduce cancellations, and make better operational decisions.
Before performing detailed analysis, key performance indicators were calculated to provide a
high-level overview of the hotel booking dataset.

The main KPIs analysed were:
• Total valid bookings after data cleaning.
• Overall cancellation rate.
• Average booking lead time.
• Average Daily Rate (ADR).
These indicators provide an overall understanding of hotel booking behaviour and create a
baseline for the detailed analysis presented in the following sections.
The remaining analysis investigates how booking patterns, hotel type, stay duration, and
lead time are related to customer cancellation behaviour


##2. Hotel Booking Analysis

Which Hotel Type Is Booked More Often?
• The analysis shows that City Hotel is booked more often than Resort Hotel.
• City Hotel represents approximately 60.98% of total bookings, while Resort Hotel
represents approximately 39.02%.
• This means that City Hotel received a larger share of customer bookings in this
dataset.
• One possible reason is that City Hotels can be useful for different types of travel, such
as business trips, short visits, events, and tourism. Resort Hotels may depend more
on leisure travel and vacation periods.

###Monthly Booking Pattern

• I also analysed how the number of bookings changes during different months.
• The booking numbers show that hotel demand changes throughout the year. Both
City Hotel and Resort Hotel have months with higher and lower booking demand.
• For both hotel types, October had the highest number of bookings.
